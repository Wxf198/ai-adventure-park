🔒 Privacy Policy & Children's Privacy Statement

Last updated: May 9, 2026

✅ COPPA Compliant ✅ No Data Collection ✅ No Ads · Forever

📋 Summary for Parents (Plain English)

This website is an educational resource about artificial intelligence for children aged 8–14. Here is what you need to know in one breath:

  • We collect zero personal information. No names, no emails, no locations, nothing.
  • No accounts, no logins, no passwords. Your child never creates an account here.
  • No comments, no chat, no user-generated content. There is no way for your child to share anything with strangers on this site.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics. We do not use Google Analytics, Baidu Tongji, Facebook Pixel, or any other tracking technology.
  • No cookies for tracking. The only browser storage used is localStorage, which stays entirely on your device and is never sent anywhere.
  • No AI chat or user input sent to servers. All interactive features run entirely inside your browser. Nothing your child types or draws leaves their device.

🧒 Children's Online Privacy (COPPA & International)

AI Adventure Park complies with:

  • COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) — United States
  • 儿童个人信息网络保护规定 — China (Regulations on the Protection of Children's Personal Information Online)
  • GDPR-K — European Union (General Data Protection Regulation, as it applies to children)

The simplest way to comply with all of these at once is what we've done: never collect any personal information in the first place.

We do not require, request, or accept any personal information from anyone, of any age. If you believe your child has provided personal information to us (which shouldn't be possible by design), please contact us immediately and we will ensure it is destroyed.

💾 What Is localStorage and Why Do We Use It?

To make the learning experience fun, we use a browser feature called localStorage to save your child's progress. Here's exactly what it stores, and why it stays private:

  • Which lessons have been completed — so the progress bar updates
  • AI invention ideas your child writes in Lesson 6 — so they can see their list of inventions

This data never leaves your device. localStorage is a built-in browser feature that stores data only on the device itself. It is not transmitted to any server. Other websites cannot read it. It can be cleared at any time through your browser settings.

🔗 External Links

Our Resources section links to educational websites we recommend: Scratch (scratch.mit.edu), Code.org, and Quick, Draw! (quickdraw.withgoogle.com). These are separate websites with their own privacy policies. We encourage parents to review them.

🔐 Security

This website is served exclusively over HTTPS, which encrypts all data in transit. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a static hosting service — there is no backend server, no database, and no API that could store or process personal data.
