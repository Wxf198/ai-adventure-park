1. From the Human Brain to the AI Brain

🧬 Neurons in the Human Brain

Your brain has about 86 billion neurons. Each neuron is like a tiny lightbulb 💡, connected to other neurons through synapses (wires).

When you see a cat 🐱: Light enters your eyes → retinal neurons activate → signals travel through layers of neurons → the cerebral cortex decides: "That's a cat!"

This whole process takes less than half a second!

🤖 The "Artificial Neuron" in AI

Scientists modeled the structure of the human brain and designed Artificial Neural Networks:

Each artificial neuron receives signals from the previous layer → performs simple math → passes the result to the next layer.
While each neuron does something very simple, when thousands are connected together, they can accomplish incredibly complex tasks!
