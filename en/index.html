✨ What is AI? In Three Sentences

Understanding artificial intelligence the kid-friendly way

🧠

A Program That Learns

AI isn't a real person — it's a clever computer program. Show it lots of examples, and it figures out patterns on its own.

🐶

Like Training a Puppy

Just like teaching a puppy to "sit" takes practice, training AI takes repetition. This is called Machine Learning.

🔧

Just a Tool

AI is like a hammer 🔨 or paintbrush 🖌️ — it's only as good as the person using it. Learning to use AI wisely is a superpower!

🌍

Already Everywhere

Face unlock, voice assistants, video recommendations, navigation maps — AI is already part of your daily life!
