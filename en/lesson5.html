AI Makes Mistakes Too!

Discover AI's limitations -- bias, blind spots, and why human oversight matters

Knowledge

1. AI Isn't Perfect

AI's Four "Flaws"

😅Only Learns, Doesn't ThinkAI doesn't understand "happy" or "sad" -- it just finds patterns in data and imitates them
📊Depends on Data QualityBad training data = AI learns bad things. (Garbage in = Garbage out)
🎭Can Have BiasIf you only show AI certain types of data, it will make mistakes on things it hasn't seen
🔋Very Energy-HungryTraining one large AI can use as much electricity as an average household uses in 100 years
Experiment

2. Bias Experiment -- See It Yourself

Same algorithm, different training data -- completely different results

✅ Fair Training

Cats and dogs each make up half of the data

⚠️ Biased Training

Only show AI orange cats

🚨 See what happened? The biased AI got every non-orange animal wrong!
In the real world, if you train AI this way -- for example, only training face recognition on one skin tone -- it would be deeply unfair to others.

📰 Real-World AI Bias Cases

  • 🔍 A hiring AI learned to discriminate against women because historical data showed more male engineers
  • 👁️ A face recognition system had an error rate 10 times higher for darker skin tones than lighter ones
  • 📱 A phone's face unlock was fooled by twins unlocking each other's phones
💡 Lesson: AI is only as good as its training data and designers. That's why learning AI ethics is just as important as learning AI -- to make sure AI is fair for everyone.
